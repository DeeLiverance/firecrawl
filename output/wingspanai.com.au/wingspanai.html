Cookie Policy
=============

Effective Date: 22 April 2025  | Last Updated: January 2026

WingSpanAi uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience while you navigate our website.

WingSpanAi believes in transparency about our collection and use of data. This policy explains how and when WingSpanAi uses cookies and similar technologies. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning set forth in our Privacy Policy.

What is a cookie?
-----------------

Cookies are small text files sent by us to your computer or mobile device that enable WingSpanAi features and functionality. They are unique to your account or browser.

**Session Cookies** last only while your browser is open and are deleted when you close it.

**Persistent Cookies** remain until you or your browser delete them or they expire.

Does WingSpanAi use cookies?
----------------------------

Yes. We use cookies and similar technologies (e.g., single‑pixel GIFs, web beacons) on domains operated by WingSpanAi and its affiliates (collectively, the “Sites”). We use both session‑based and persistent cookies, including first‑ and third‑party cookies (e.g., Google Analytics).

How does WingSpanAi use cookies?
--------------------------------

Authentication  
Remember you’re signed in and which workspaces you’ve accessed.

Security  
Enable and support security features, detect malicious activity.

Preferences & Features  
Store your language, communication preferences, and form‑fill data.

Performance, Analytics & Research  
Understand Site/Service usage, measure performance, improve features.

Marketing  
Deliver and track marketing campaigns; partners may set cookies subject to their own policies.

What third-party cookies do we use?
-----------------------------------

Our primary third‑party cookies are set by Google Analytics. A full list appears in Appendix A below. We do our best to keep this list current, but names and numbers may change.

Advertising & Tracking
----------------------

Cookies and ad technologies (beacons, pixels, tags) help WingSpanAi market effectively and perform aggregated auditing, research, and reporting.

Consent Management
------------------

We use a Consent Management Platform (CMP) to obtain, record, and manage your cookie preferences.

*   **Consent Banner:** Displays on first visit, requiring opt-in for non-essential cookies.
*   **Audit Trail:** Consent timestamps and versions are logged for 2 years.
*   **Withdrawal:** You can change or withdraw consent at any time via the [Cookie Preferences Center](https://wingspanai.com.au/cookie-manager/)

Managing or Disabling Cookies
-----------------------------

Access our Cookie Manager via the footer of any WingspanAi webpage.  
Or adjust your browser settings (Help, Tools, or Preferences menus) to block or alert you about cookies.

_Note: Blocking certain cookies may degrade your experience or prevent features from working._

Disabling a cookie stops future collection, but data collected before disabling may still be processed.

Do Not Track Signals
--------------------

We do not alter our data collection and use practices in response to browser “Do Not Track” signals.
